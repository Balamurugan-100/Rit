## The Plan for rit

The plan for rit is to build a basic version of Git in Rust. I want to avoid relying too much on AI, because doing so would weaken my instincts as a developer. Instead, I will plan everything before I start, which will help me improve at what I do.

The goal is not to build something that replaces Git, but to understand the foundations of the tool I use every day by rebuilding a basic version of it in a language I want to learn. This will strengthen my instincts as a developer, help me understand the problems involved, and give me more perspectives as I learn more.

Documenting the process as I go is also a main goal.
